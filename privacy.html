Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information QuizzifyTech (“we”, “us”) collects when you use quizzifytech.com, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game never asks players for an email address or any account, we count plays anonymously, we may use Google Analytics to see how the site is used, and if ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies to show them.

1. Information saved in your browser

The game uses your browser’s local storage (similar to cookies) to work. This stays on your device:

WhatWhy
Your game progress, scores, streak and settingsSo you can continue where you left off
A random visitor IDSo we can count visitors without knowing who you are
Whether you have closed the privacy noticeSo it does not keep showing

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for quizzifytech.com. This also resets your progress.

2. Anonymous play statistics

To understand how the game is used, the site sends simple events to our database (hosted by Supabase): a page visit, starting a puzzle, finding or missing a bug, and sharing a result. Each event includes the random visitor ID, the time, the website that linked you here (for example linkedin.com) and, for visits, your browser type. We do not store your name, email address or IP address with these events, and we do not use them to identify you.

3. No email or account

QuizzifyTech has no sign-up and no sign-in. The game never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details. If you email us yourself, we only use your email address to reply.

4. Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep QuizzifyTech free, we may show ads served by Google AdSense. When ads are on:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised ads at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used, through Google’s consent message. You can change your choice later using the privacy settings button shown on the page.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how people find and use the site: pages viewed, the puzzles started, found or missed, shares, and general device, browser and country information. Google Analytics uses cookies (such as _ga) and a random ID to tell visits apart. It does not give us your name or email address, and Google Analytics does not store full IP addresses.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, analytics cookies are only used after you agree in the consent message. Until then, Google receives only basic signals without cookies.
  • You can block Google Analytics on all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Read Google’s Privacy Policy to learn more.

6. Other services the site loads

  • Google Tag Manager loads Google Analytics and, if used, other Google tags.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. Your browser contacts Google to download them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • Share buttons open LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, Telegram or Reddit only when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

7. How long we keep data

Anonymous play statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for improving the game, and at most 2 years.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Email hello@quizzifytech.com and we will help. Players hold no account with us, so we hold nothing that identifies you, but we can still delete events linked to your visitor ID if you send it to us.

9. Children

QuizzifyTech is made for adults and students learning software testing. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@quizzifytech.com or use the Contact page.
