About QuizzifyTech

Free daily tech quizzes for testers and developers

QuizzifyTech is a free website where you practise finding software bugs. Every puzzle shows a screen from a made-up app, such as a shopping cart, a banking page, an API response or a database table. Each screen hides exactly one bug. You tap the part you think is wrong, and you get three tries. After each puzzle you read a short bug report that explains what was wrong, what the app should have done and why it matters to real users.

Why it exists

Good testers are not born with a sharp eye for defects. They build it by seeing many bugs, again and again. Most of us only see the bugs in the one product we work on. QuizzifyTech gives you a few minutes of varied, real-world bugs every day, so you build that eye faster: wrong totals, broken labels, impossible dates, leaked data, slow pages, confusing AI answers and more.

What you will find here

  • Today’s Quiz: 8 new bugs every day, from easy to hard. Everyone in the world gets the same set, so you can compare scores with friends and teammates.
  • Practice: every puzzle in the library, sorted into Beginner, Intermediate and Experienced levels.
  • Tracks: focus on Frontend, Backend, API, Database or AI testing.
  • Bug reports: each puzzle ends with steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, a severity and a testing tip you can use at work. You can read all of them in the Bug library.

Who it is for

  • Manual and automation testers who want to sharpen their attention to detail.
  • Students and freshers preparing for QA and software testing interviews.
  • Developers who want to think like a tester before they ship.
  • QA leads who want a quick daily warm-up for their team.

How the puzzles are made

Each puzzle is based on a type of bug that happens in real products: rounding errors in prices, off-by-one mistakes in pages and dates, missing validation, security slips such as tokens in a URL, and AI answers that make things up. The apps, people and companies on the screens are invented. Every puzzle is checked so that it has one clear bug and a fair explanation.

Is it free?

Yes. QuizzifyTech is free to play and needs no sign-up. To keep it free, the site may show ads from Google AdSense. You can read how that works in our Privacy Policy.

Who runs it
