Bugspot

What is Bugspot?

Bugspot is a free daily spot-the-bug game for QA testers, software testers and developers. Each puzzle shows a screen from a made-up app with one defect hiding in plain sight: a wrong total, a broken label, a date that cannot be right. Tap the bug before your three tries run out, then read a short bug report that explains what was wrong and why it matters.

How to play

  • Every day there are 8 new bugs, from easy to hard, across frontend, backend, API, database and AI testing. Everyone gets the same set.
  • Tap the part of the screen you think is wrong. You have three tries, and a hint if you need it.
  • Score points: 100 if you find the bug on the first try, 60 on the second, 30 on the third. A hint costs 20 points. The best daily score is 800.
  • Share your result and keep your streak going. A new set arrives at midnight.
  • Want more? Practice mode has every bug, sorted into Beginner, Intermediate and Experienced levels. Pick a track to practise only API, database or AI bugs.

Who is it for?

Manual and automation testers who want to sharpen their eye for detail, people preparing for QA interviews, and teams who want a quick daily warm-up. It is free and needs no sign-up.
