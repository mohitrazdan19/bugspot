AI testing · Support bot

Bot reply shows raw Markdown (**, ##, -) instead of formatting

P4 · CosmeticBeginnerSupport bot

Want to find it yourself first? This page gives away the answer. Play this bug on a fake app screen and see if you can spot it in three tries.

Spot this bug

The clue

Look at how the text is formatted.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Ask the bot “Where is my order?”
  2. Look at the formatting of the reply

Expected result

“Order status” in bold, a proper heading and bullet

Actual result

Literal **Order status:**, ## Next steps and - characters

Why it matters

This is a cosmetic (P4) bug. It is a small visual or wording problem. It is cheap to fix and worth reporting, because many small slips add up to a product that feels unfinished.

Testing tip

Models often answer in Markdown. Check the app renders it, or tell the model to reply in plain text.

How to find bugs like this

AI testing bugs come from chatbots and AI features: made-up facts, answers that change, prompt injection, leaked instructions and bias in what the model says. When you test, try these checks:

  • Ask the same question several times and in different words.
  • Hide instructions inside documents and inputs.
  • Ask about data the user should not see.
  • Check every fact, number and source the AI gives.
