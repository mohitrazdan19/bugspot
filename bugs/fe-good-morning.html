App says “Good morning” at 9:41 PM

P4 · CosmeticBeginnerHome

Want to find it yourself first? This page gives away the answer. Play this bug on a fake app screen and see if you can spot it in three tries.

Spot this bug

The clue

Check the time in the corner.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open the app late in the evening
  2. Read the greeting on Home

Expected result

Good evening, Riya

Actual result

Good morning, Riya at 21:41

Why it matters

This is a cosmetic (P4) bug. It is a small visual or wording problem. It is cheap to fix and worth reporting, because many small slips add up to a product that feels unfinished.

Testing tip

Time-based text often uses the server’s time zone or a fixed value. Test greetings at night and in other time zones.

How to find bugs like this

Frontend bugs are the ones users see on the screen: wrong totals, labels that do not match, broken layouts, dates that cannot be right and buttons that say the wrong thing. When you test, try these checks:

  • Add up every total and compare numbers that should match.
  • Read every label, plural and date out loud.
  • Try the smallest and largest values a field allows.
  • Check the screen on a small phone and with long text.
