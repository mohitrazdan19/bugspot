102 real-world software bugs, explained

Every puzzle on QuizzifyTech is based on a kind of bug that happens in real products. This library lists all of them with a full bug report: steps to reproduce, the expected and actual result, how serious it is and a tip you can use when you test. Use it to prepare for QA interviews, to learn what to look for in a new area, or to pick bugs to discuss with your team.

Each page gives away the answer, so if you want a challenge, tap Spot this bug on the page and try to find it first. New bugs arrive in Today’s Quiz every day.

Severity in one line

  • P1 Critical: loses money, leaks data or blocks a key task.
  • P2 Major: wrong information or a broken step many users will hit.
  • P3 Minor: does not stop anyone, but hurts trust.
  • P4 Cosmetic: a small visual or wording slip.
