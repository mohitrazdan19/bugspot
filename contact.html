What to write to us about

  • A mistake in a puzzle. Tell us the date of Today’s Quiz or the puzzle title, and what you think is wrong. A screenshot helps.
  • An idea for a new bug. Describe a bug you have seen in real life. We may turn it into a puzzle (we never use your company’s name or real data).
  • Team and training use. If you lead a QA team and want to use QuizzifyTech for practice or onboarding, we would love to hear from you.
  • Privacy questions. To ask what data we hold or to have it deleted, see our Privacy Policy and email us.
  • Advertising and partnerships. Questions about the site, ads or working together.

Something is broken?

Please tell us which device and browser you use (for example “Android phone, Chrome”) and what you tapped before it went wrong. You do not need to clear anything first. Your progress is saved in your browser, and we can usually fix things without you losing it.
